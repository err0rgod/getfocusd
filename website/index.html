Features

Everything you need to stay focused

Six tools that work together to protect your attention.

Sealed Blocks

Pick a site, set a timer. Until it expires, the site never loads. Early removal requires passing an unlock barrier.

Pomodoro Focus Timer

Work/break cycles with a configurable focus list. Distracting sites are sealed during work; breaks lift the block.

Daily Budgets

Set a per-site daily minute allowance. A local heartbeat tracks your active time; hit the limit and the site is blocked until midnight.

Mindful Pause

Chosen sites get a full-screen breathing countdown before they load, giving you a moment to reconsider.

Shorts Blocker

Redirects /shorts/ URLs to the YouTube homepage and hides Shorts shelves, sidebar entries, and links.

Insights

Blocked-attempt counts, 7-day chart, top distracting sites, streaks, focus minutes, and per-site time today.

How it works

Three steps to reclaim your time

1

Pick a site & time

Click the extension icon on any distracting tab. Choose a duration — 30 minutes, 2 hours, whatever you need.

2

It’s sealed

The site redirects to a block page with a live countdown. Removing it early means typing a motivational quote or entering a friend-set password.

3

Timer ends, you’re free

When the countdown hits zero, the block lifts automatically. You kept your commitment — no willpower needed.
